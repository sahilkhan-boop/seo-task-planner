Benchmarks

Every task the platform generates from GA4/GSC data (phase 3-4) is a metric compared against one of these thresholds. CTR is segmented by average position because expected CTR at position 2 and position 15 are very different — flag a page against the wrong segment and you'll chase pages that are actually fine.
